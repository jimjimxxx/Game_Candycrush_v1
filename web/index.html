How to play
  • Click a candy, then an adjacent one, to swap them. On a touch screen you can also swipe.
  • Lining up three or more of the same kind clears them; candies fall and new ones drop in, which can clear again (cascades).
  • A swap that makes no line is undone but still uses one of your 15 moves.
  • Each clearing wave scores candies × multiplier: 3–6 ×1, 7–12 ×2, 13+ ×5.
  • The hint and the agent both pick the move that clears the most candies right away.
  • Add ?seed=1 to the address to replay the same game.
